# Knowledge seed list

This is a candidate collection map. Entries are not online-verified until their edition, author, link, DOI, license, and current applicability are checked.

## Relationship science

- Gottman relationship research and marriage communication.
- Sue Johnson and emotion-focused couples work.
- Adult attachment and interdependence research.

## Communication and negotiation

- Nonviolent Communication.
- Difficult Conversations.
- Fight Right and repair after conflict.
- Getting to Yes and boundary-setting methods.

## Personality, sexuality, and intimacy

- Big Five research and limits of personality typologies.
- Emily Nagoski's work on desire, sexual communication, and bodily autonomy.
- Consent, contraception, sexual health, and desire discrepancy research.

## Marriage and family

- Premarital relationship education.
- Bowen family systems, emotional differentiation, and intergenerational boundaries.
- Shared finances, household labor, parenting, and in-law negotiation.

## Law, safety, and social context

- Current Chinese marriage and family law.
- Domestic violence, stalking, fraud, evidence preservation, and crisis pathways.
- Marriage change, population, urbanization, platform dating, gendered work, and care labor.

## Field cases

- Creator videos about dating, family negotiation, bride price, conflict, and breakups.
- User-provided cases and outcome reviews.

## Collection rule

The catalog separates L1 research and law, L2 professional material, L3 creator experience, and L4 personal cases. A creator tactic enters the reusable playbook only after its context, limits, counterexample, and observed outcome are recorded.
