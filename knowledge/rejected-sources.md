## Use rule

- Search results must be checked for retraction, correction, expression of concern, and duplicate publication before extraction.
- A retracted paper stays excluded even if its abstract remains visible or another paper cites it.
- If only part of a source is affected, record the publisher's exact notice before deciding whether any unaffected material may be used.
