## Use rules

- Books supply concepts, questions, exercises, and language; papers and official sources determine factual confidence.
- A memorable phrase from a book is not a universal decision rule.
- When two frameworks disagree, present the disagreement and choose based on the user's goal, context, evidence quality, and reversibility.
- Do not use MBTI, attachment labels, or a named relationship framework as a substitute for observable behavior.
