# Demand/withdraw conflict playbook

## Evidence base

Schrodt, Witt & Shimkowski (2014), DOI 10.1080/03637751.2013.813632, meta-analyzed 74 studies and found a moderate association between demand/withdraw interaction and worse individual, relational, and communicative outcomes. This association does not prove causation or determine which partner is right.

## Use this playbook when

One person repeatedly presses for an answer, raises intensity, repeats accusations, or follows the other person, while the other repeatedly becomes silent, changes the subject, delays indefinitely, leaves, or stops responding. Describe actions rather than labeling someone “avoidant,” “controlling,” or a personality type.

## Decision sequence

1. Stop arguing about the underlying issue for the moment and name the loop with one observable example.
2. Ask whether either person is too activated to continue. If yes, take a time-limited pause of 20 minutes to 24 hours depending on intensity.
3. Before separating, set an exact return time and channel. A pause without a return commitment counts as unresolved withdrawal.
4. At the return, discuss one decision only. Each person gets: one fact, one impact, one concrete request, and one question.
5. Write the decision, owner, deadline, and next review time. If no agreement is possible, record the two positions and choose whether to defer, test one reversible option, or escalate to a neutral professional.

## Suggested scripts

To interrupt the loop:

> 我们现在进入了“我越追问、你越不说；你越不说、我越追问”的循环。继续下去只会让强度更高。我想先停 40 分钟，今晚 9 点回到客厅，只谈这一个问题：___。到时我们各说一个事实、一个影响和一个具体请求。

When the other person requests a pause:

> 可以暂停，但我需要一个明确的回来时间。你选今晚 9 点或明早 10 点；如果两个时间都不行，请给出 24 小时内的具体时间。

When pressure continues during the pause:

> 我已经承诺___点回来谈。现在继续追问会破坏这次暂停。我会在约定时间出现；在那之前不继续讨论。

## Reaction branches

| Reaction | Response | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Accepts the pause and return time | Confirm topic, time, and place in one sentence | Regulate separately; return on time |
| Says “you are escaping” | “暂停不是取消，我把返回时间写清楚并会准时回来。” | Offer two return times, choose one |
| Refuses every return time | “如果 24 小时内没有可谈时间，我会把这视为拒绝共同解决问题。” | Stop chasing; apply the stated decision consequence |
| Continues messaging or following | Repeat the boundary once and disengage from the topic | Move to a safe/private space if needed |
| Returns but reopens many issues | “今天只处理___，其他问题记入下一次清单。” | Park extra issues in writing |
| Both return but cannot speak without escalation | End the session after one attempt | Use written exchange or a qualified couples professional if safe |

## Stop conditions

- threats, intimidation, blocking exits, destroying property, coercion, stalking, or physical violence;
- a pause is used repeatedly to punish, disappear, or avoid every consequential conversation;
- one person breaks agreed return times repeatedly without proposing replacements;
- conflict intensity makes either person unable to stay safe or understand the discussion.

When a stop condition involves safety, use the safety and legal protocol rather than this communication exercise.
