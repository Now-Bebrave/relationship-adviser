# Attachment-pattern conversation playbook

## Evidence base

Candel & Turliuc (2019), DOI 10.1016/j.paid.2019.04.037, found in a meta-analysis that attachment anxiety and avoidance were associated with lower relationship satisfaction, with Actor effects stronger than partner effects. The evidence supports examining patterns; it does not support diagnosing a fixed “attachment type” from a few messages.

## Map the episode before choosing advice

Write one recent episode in five lines:

1. **Trigger:** the observable event, such as a delayed reply or request for space.
2. **Interpretation:** the meaning assigned, such as “I am being abandoned” or “I am being controlled.”
3. **Protection action:** repeated contact, testing, criticism, silence, intellectualizing, or leaving.
4. **Partner response:** reassurance, pursuit, withdrawal, anger, or compliance.
5. **Consequence:** immediate relief and the cost during the next 24 hours.

Intervene at the protection action, because the label itself does not specify what to do.

## Concrete replacements

- Pursuit or reassurance seeking: make one specific request with a response window, then stop adding tests or accusations.
- Withdrawal: ask for a time-limited pause and state the exact return time before leaving the conversation.
- Ambiguous contact: agree on a minimum communication expectation for predictable situations such as work, travel, or conflict.
- Repeated interpretation disputes: separate “what happened,” “what I feared it meant,” and “what I need next time.”

Suggested script:

> 刚才发生的是___。我脑中把它理解成___，所以我做了___，结果你又___。我不想继续这个循环。下次我会改成___；我需要你在___时间内明确告诉我___。你能否做到？

## Reaction branches

| Reaction | Response | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 对方接受具体约定 | “我们把时间和动作写清楚，试两周再看。” | 只观察履约，不反复测爱意 |
| “你太黏/太冷漠” | “先不贴标签，请指出刚才哪个动作造成了什么影响。” | 返回五行事件图 |
| 要求无限即时回应 | “我能承诺___内回复，不能承诺随时在线。” | 设可持续响应窗口 |
| 要求无限期空间 | “可以暂停，但需要具体返回时间；没有返回安排不算解决。” | 最长窗口按事件风险确定 |
| 用童年或依恋标签免责 | “原因可以解释反应，但仍需要改变造成伤害的行为。” | 要求一个可验证替代动作 |
| 约定持续不履行 | “我们已经试了___次，下一步不再重复口头保证。” | 降低投入或进入关系可行性评估 |

## Stop conditions

- 用“焦虑型”合理化监控、强迫联系、威胁或侵犯隐私；
- 用“回避型”合理化失联、惩罚性沉默、隐瞒或拒绝所有重要讨论；
- 出现暴力、跟踪、限制自由或其他安全风险；
- 多次明确约定后仍只有解释，没有行为变化。
