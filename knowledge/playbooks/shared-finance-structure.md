# Shared-finance structure playbook

## Evidence base

Olson et al. (2023), DOI 10.1093/jcr/ucad020, conducted a six-wave randomized longitudinal experiment with engaged or newlywed couples who initially had separate accounts. Couples assigned to merge funds maintained stronger relationship quality over two years than separate-account and no-intervention groups. The result does not establish one safe structure for every couple.

## Safety screen before merging

Do not merge funds while any of these remain unresolved: hidden debt, gambling or uncontrolled spending, identity misuse, financial control, blocked account access, threats, unstable immigration or housing dependence, active separation planning, or inability to retrieve personal documents and emergency money.

## Choose the structure by purpose

1. List all income, debt, recurring obligations, dependents, assets, credit exposure, and legal ownership.
2. Agree on shared goals, required bills, individual discretion, reporting threshold, and what requires two approvals.
3. Compare three structures:
   - full joint: simplest shared visibility and goals, highest exposure to the other's actions;
   - hybrid: joint operating account plus individual accounts, clearer autonomy but more transfers and rules;
   - separate: strongest operational separation, but shared goals and bills need explicit coordination.
4. Choose one structure for a stated reason. Do not copy the experimental result beyond its sample and treatment.
5. Set access, alerts, contribution formula, spending threshold, emergency reserve, and monthly review date in writing.
6. Review after three months using missed bills, savings progress, conflict, transparency, and individual access—not only feelings.

Suggested script:

> 我们先不争论“共同账户才算信任”。我要解决的是___。在决定前，我们互相披露收入、债务、义务和信用风险，再比较全共同、混合和分开三种结构。无论选哪种，都必须保留双方查看记录、基本生活和紧急处理的能力。

## Reaction branches

| Reaction | Response | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| “爱我就全部合并” | “账户结构是风险和协作安排，不是感情测试。” | 回到目标与安全清单 |
| 拒绝披露债务 | “数字不透明前不合并、不担保、不新增共同负债。” | 暂停财务绑定 |
| 担心失去自由 | “先约定个人可自由支配额度和双方对等访问权。” | 优先比较混合结构 |
| 收入差距很大 | “比较固定金额、收入比例和必要支出后的可支配比例。” | 选规则并试行三个月 |
| 父母或前段关系有义务 | “把金额、期限和优先级写入共同预算。” | 不接受无限、未披露承诺 |
| 合并后冲突增加 | “检查是透明带来旧问题，还是结构本身造成控制和摩擦。” | 修规则；出现控制则拆分并保护资金 |

## Stop conditions

- financial control：限制基本生活费、没收收入、单方面封锁账户或用钱威胁；
- 未经同意新增共同债务、担保、抵押或大额转账；
- 伪造、隐瞒或拒绝提供会改变共同风险的关键信息；
- 合并会妨碍安全离开、就医、住房或法律求助。
