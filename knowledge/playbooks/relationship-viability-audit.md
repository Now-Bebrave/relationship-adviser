# Relationship viability audit

## Evidence base

Le et al. (2010), DOI 10.1111/j.1475-6811.2010.01285.x, synthesized 137 samples with 37,761 participants in nonmarital relationships. Commitment, love, inclusion of the partner in the self, and dependence were among the strongest dissolution predictors. These are group-level predictors, not an individual breakup calculator.

## Run a 30-day evidence audit

For each domain, record a current statement, one observable behavior, and whether it changed during the month:

| Domain | Evidence question |
|---|---|
| Commitment | Does each person protect the relationship when doing so has a real but reasonable cost? |
| Affection and care | Is warmth expressed in forms the other person can actually receive? |
| Shared identity | Are important plans framed as “we” while preserving each person's autonomy? |
| Dependence and alternatives | Is the relationship chosen for its value, or maintained mainly from fear, sunk cost, housing, money, or lack of alternatives? |
| Repair capacity | After conflict, does the same problem produce a concrete change and follow-up? |
| Future compatibility | Are marriage, children, location, money, fidelity, family boundaries, and lifestyle plans mutually workable? |

## Decision rule

- **Continue and repair:** both people explicitly choose the relationship, agree on the same problem, and complete observable changes during the review period.
- **Pause escalation:** affection exists but commitment, transparency, or future plans remain unclear; do not add cohabitation, pregnancy, marriage, joint debt, or large transfers while testing.
- **Exit planning:** one person clearly declines the requested relationship, repeatedly deceives, refuses every repair process, or violates a non-negotiable boundary.

Suggested script:

> 我不想再靠一两次情绪高低判断关系。接下来 30 天我们只验证三件事：___、___、___。每件事写清动作和日期。到___日，如果双方都完成，我们继续推进；如果仍只有承诺没有行动，我会停止___并做下一步决定。

## Reaction branches

| Reaction | Response | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 愿意共同验证 | 明确最多三个行为与日期 | 每周只复盘一次 |
| “你不信任我” | “信任需要可观察的一致性，这个过程也约束我。” | 双方承担对等任务 |
| 只重复“我爱你” | “感情很重要，我还需要看到___这个具体选择。” | 要求行为和期限 |
| 拒绝期限 | “没有期限就无法区分困难和无限拖延。” | 给一个合理最终日期 |
| 突然短期表现很好 | “我认可变化，我们按原定周期看稳定性。” | 不因一周改善立刻升级承诺 |
| 明确不愿继续 | “我接受你的决定，不再说服；接下来只处理交接和边界。” | 进入分手执行方案 |

## Stop conditions

- 安全、暴力、强迫、跟踪或重大欺骗问题不进入30天实验，直接使用安全或退出方案；
- 为了证明关系而新增共同债务、共同产权、怀孕或大额转账；
- 一方利用审查期索取更多投入，却拒绝对等透明和行动；
- 审查结束后继续无限延长，没有新增信息或行为变化。
