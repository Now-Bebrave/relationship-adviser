# Reproductive autonomy and consent playbook

## Evidence base

Grace & Anderson (2018), DOI 10.1177/1524838016663935, systematically reviewed 27 studies on reproductive coercion. It distinguishes birth-control sabotage, pregnancy coercion, and control over pregnancy outcomes, and reports frequent overlap with intimate-partner violence. Much of the evidence is descriptive, so use it to identify safety variables rather than assign probabilities.

## Classify before discussing

Ask privately and safely:

1. Can each person say yes, no, or not now to sex without punishment, threats, withdrawal of necessities, or retaliation?
2. Does each person control their own contraception, medical information, pregnancy decision, and access to care?
3. Has anyone removed, damaged, hidden, lied about, or interfered with a condom or contraceptive method?
4. Has anyone threatened abandonment, violence, financial punishment, exposure, or family consequences over pregnancy or abortion decisions?

If any answer suggests coercion, stop treating the issue as a negotiation problem. Prioritize private support, medical care, and safety planning.

## Safe conversation when no coercion is present

1. Discuss whether pregnancy is wanted now, later, or not at all.
2. Agree on the method, STI-testing and exclusivity expectations, what happens if a method fails, and who can access medical information.
3. Confirm consent at the time of sexual activity; prior agreement or relationship status does not replace current consent.
4. Revisit after changes in health, medication, relationship status, or pregnancy goals.

Suggested script:

> 生育和避孕是我们两个人都要有自主决定权的事。我们分别说清楚现在想要什么、不能接受什么、避孕失败时怎么处理。任何人都可以随时暂停性行为或更换方案，不用靠威胁、隐瞒或惩罚来推进。

## If coercion is suspected

- 如果对质可能增加危险，不要单独对质疑似施害者。
- Use a private device or safe channel to contact a qualified healthcare provider, domestic-violence service, or trusted person; ask about confidential care and local options.
- Tell the provider what happened, including condom interference, contraception sabotage, pregnancy pressure, threats, or forced decisions; ask about STI testing, emergency contraception, pregnancy care, and safety planning as relevant.
- Preserve messages, medical records, dates, and incidents only when doing so is safe. Do not create a discoverable record that increases immediate danger.
- If there is immediate danger, leave if possible and contact local emergency services.

## Reaction branches

| Reaction | Response | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 对方愿意平等讨论 | “我们把各自的意愿、边界和失败预案写清楚。” | 选择双方都能自主执行的方案 |
| 对方说“情侣不需要谈同意” | “关系状态不替代当下同意，任何人都能暂停。” | 暂停相关行为，观察是否尊重边界 |
| 对方要求隐瞒或停止避孕 | “这是我的身体和生育决定，我不会在压力下同意。” | 保持私密医疗和支持渠道 |
| 发现套子被动过或避孕被干扰 | 不在现场争辩或单独对质 | 先安全、医疗、记录和外部支持 |
| 对方用分手、钱或家庭施压 | “威胁不能构成同意。” | 暂停生育/性行为决定，评估安全与关系退出 |
| 对方否认且你仍不安全 | 不以说服对方为目标 | 按安全计划求助，不继续谈判 |

## 停止条件

- 性行为没有自由、持续和可撤回的同意；
- 避孕或安全措施被破坏、隐瞒或强行取消；
- 以暴力、威胁、金钱、住房、身份、子女或家庭压力逼迫生育或终止妊娠；
- 对方跟踪、限制就医、没收证件或阻止求助；
- 任何即时人身危险。

本方案不能替代当地医疗、法律或危机服务的个案意见。涉及紧急避孕、暴露后预防、妊娠、伤害或即时危险时，应尽快联系当地专业机构。
