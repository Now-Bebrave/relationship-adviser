# Dyadic coping playbook

## Evidence base

Falconier & Kuhn (2019), DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2019.00571, reviewed 139 studies and found broad relational benefits associated with positive dyadic coping in Western couples. It distinguishes stress communication, supportive or empathic responses, delegated help, active engagement, collaborative coping, and negative forms such as overprotection, hostility, and disengaged avoidance.

## First classify the stressor

Ask one question before advising:

> 这件事主要来自关系外部/共同生活压力，还是就是我们彼此之间的伤害与冲突？

- **External or shared stress:** unemployment, illness, relocation, caregiving, exams, family crisis, or debt. Use coordinated coping.
- **Within-relationship harm:** betrayal, coercion, violence, repeated deception, or a boundary violation. Do not label forced closeness “dyadic coping”; first establish safety, accountability, and whether joint work is voluntary.

## Four-step coordinated response

1. **Stress communication:** state the stressor, intensity from 0–10, and the kind of help wanted.
2. **Match the response:** listening, emotional reassurance, a practical task, delegated responsibility, or joint planning.
3. **Name limits:** say what support is not wanted, what each person can realistically provide, and when to seek outside help.
4. **Review:** after 24–72 hours, check whether the response reduced load, increased connection, or created control and resentment.

Suggested script:

> 这周我面对的是___，强度大约___分。我现在需要你做的是___，不需要你做的是___。你能承担到___程度吗？我们在___时间复盘，如果不够就调整或找外部支持。

## Reaction branches

| Reaction | Response | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 对方愿意协作 | “我确认一下：你负责___，我负责___，明天___点复盘。” | 写下责任和时间 |
| 对方只给建议 | “我现在先需要被听见/一个具体动作，不是更多方案。” | 重新指定支持类型 |
| 对方过度接管 | “谢谢你想帮忙，但这个部分仍由我决定；你可以做___。” | 保留自主权和边界 |
| 对方隐瞒压力 | “你可以暂时不说细节，但请告诉我是否需要暂停其他安排或找支持。” | 约定可接受的最低告知 |
| 两人一起陷入回避 | “我们先处理一个最小可行动任务，不把所有问题一次解决。” | 设 20 分钟行动块 |
| 因关系伤害要求立即共同面对 | “共同处理要以安全和自愿为前提；先处理事实、边界和责任。” | 必要时分开寻求支持 |

## 停止条件 / Stop conditions

- 支持被用来控制、监视、限制就医工作或剥夺决定权；
- 一方以“共同面对”为由要求继续接触施害者或隐瞒背叛；
- 出现暴力、胁迫、跟踪、报复或现实危险；
- 压力已超出伴侣能提供的支持，需要医疗、法律、危机或专业服务。
