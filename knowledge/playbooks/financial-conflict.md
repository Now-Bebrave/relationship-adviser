# Financial conflict playbook

## Evidence base

Peetz, Fisher-Skau & Joel (2024), DOI 10.1177/02654075241227454, found across two dyadic studies that financial worry was associated with perceiving less support or more negative partner behavior; the preregistered study also found evidence of perceptual bias after accounting for both partners' behavior reports. The studies are correlational.

## Prepare one worksheet

Each partner completes the same fields separately before discussion:

- monthly take-home income and volatility;
- fixed obligations, debt balance, rate, minimum payment and due date;
- variable spending from the last three months;
- emergency cash and near-term required spending;
- one shared goal and one individual priority;
- the amount each person can decide independently;
- the decision that must be made in this meeting.

## Meeting sequence

1. Read the numbers without explaining motives. Resolve mismatched balances from statements or bills.
2. Each person states one worry as a forecast: “I worry that if ___ continues, by ___ we will ___.”
3. Each person names one observed behavior without a character judgment.
4. Choose one decision only: reduce, delay, earn, refinance, disclose, or seek professional help.
5. Write the owner, amount, deadline, and review date. Use a two-week or one-month experiment for reversible choices.

Suggested opener:

> 我们先把数字和对彼此的判断分开。前 15 分钟只核对收入、余额、债务和到期日；接着各说一个担忧和一个具体请求。今天只决定___，其他问题记下来另约时间。

## Reaction branches

| Reaction | Response | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| “你就是乱花钱” | “请指出账单中的日期、金额和类别，我们处理行为，不给人格下结论。” | 核对三个月记录 |
| “我不想谈钱” | “可以不在情绪高时谈，但需要在___前完成，因为___到期。” | 给两个具体时间 |
| 数字记忆不一致 | “先不判谁撒谎，以账户和账单为准。” | 调取共同认可的原始记录 |
| 一方非常焦虑 | “先做最坏情况预算和本月必付清单，关系判断放到数字明确之后。” | 缩短到一个紧急决定 |
| 要求完全合并或完全分开 | “先说明这个结构要解决什么风险，再比较共同、个人和混合账户。” | 优先试行可逆的混合结构 |
| 发现未披露债务 | “今天暂停新增共同负债；先列全债务、账户和责任，再决定后续。” | 必要时咨询财务或法律专业人士 |

## Stop conditions

- 隐瞒债务、伪造账目、擅自使用对方身份或资产；
- 强迫借贷、控制基本生活费用、没收收入或阻止查看共同账户；
- 在关键数字仍不透明时要求结婚、共同买房、担保或大额转账；
- 任何涉及诈骗、暴力或现实安全风险的行为。
