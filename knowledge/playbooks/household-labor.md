# Household and childcare labor playbook

## Evidence base

Newkirk, Perry-Jenkins & Sayer (2017), DOI 10.1007/s11199-016-0604-3, followed 108 dual-earner new-parent couples and found that perceived fairness was related to conflict, while housework and childcare showed different patterns. The sample is narrow and does not establish one universally fair split.

## Build a complete task map

For each recurring task record five fields: owner, frequency, completion standard, deadline or trigger, and backup. Include planning and noticing work, such as checking supplies, booking appointments, remembering school messages, and arranging repairs. Keep housework, childcare, eldercare, and financial administration separate.

## Two-week allocation process

1. Each partner privately marks every task as acceptable, overloaded, or unclear.
2. Compare lists and identify the three highest-conflict tasks; do not renegotiate everything at once.
3. Assign full ownership of each selected task, including noticing, planning, execution, and follow-up.
4. Agree on a minimum acceptable standard. The owner controls method unless the result misses that standard.
5. Run the allocation for two weeks and log missed or rescued tasks without debating them in real time.
6. Review task completion, total time, disruption, and each partner's fairness rating from 0–10. Reassign one task at a time.

Suggested script:

> 我不想再用“你帮不帮忙”来谈。我们把任务的发现、计划、执行和收尾都列出来。先选最容易起冲突的三项，各自完整负责两周，然后按完成情况、耗时和双方公平感调整。

## Reaction branches

| Reaction | Response | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| “你告诉我做什么就行” | “提醒和分配本身也是任务。请完整认领一项，包括主动发现和收尾。” | 从一项低风险任务开始 |
| “我标准没你高” | “我们只约最低可接受结果和截止时间，方法由负责人决定。” | 写出可观察标准 |
| “我挣钱更多” | “收入、工时、通勤、照护和恢复时间都计入负荷，我们一起看总量。” | 比较一周总时间与不可控中断 |
| “必须绝对五五分” | “先看任务成本和双方公平感；机械数量相等不代表负荷相等。” | 用两周数据调整 |
| 一方总是补救 | “本轮记录补救次数；复盘时决定是标准不清、容量不足还是未履约。” | 不在当场扩大人格冲突 |
| 育儿预期落差 | “把照护和家务分开列，先处理夜间、就医和接送等高风险任务。” | 明确主责与备用人 |

## Stop conditions

- 一方长期拒绝承担任何可核验责任，同时要求对方继续兜底；
- 任务分配被用来控制睡眠、就医、工作、金钱或基本自由；
- 儿童、老人、宠物或家庭安全因反复失责受到现实威胁；
- 讨论升级为威胁、羞辱、破坏财物或暴力。
