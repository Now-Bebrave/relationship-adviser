## Verified scope and findings

The systematic literature review curated 45 empirical studies on social-media-induced jealousy. It groups contributors into individual factors, partner behavior, perceived rivals, and platform affordances, and describes both negative and positive consequences. The authors identify intimate-partner violence and cyberstalking as adverse consequences requiring practical attention.

The review supports a narrow decision rule: clarify the observable online behavior, the meaning assigned to it, and the boundary being requested before deciding whether a problem is trust, incompatibility, privacy intrusion, or safety risk. It does not establish a universal acceptable response time, posting rule, password policy, or gender difference.

## Limits

- This card is based on the abstract and citation metadata, not a full-text review.
- The review describes a fragmented evidence base and calls for stronger temporal and cross-cultural research.
- Jealousy is an emotion, not evidence that a partner has committed infidelity. Surveillance, threats, or forced access can themselves be harmful behavior.
