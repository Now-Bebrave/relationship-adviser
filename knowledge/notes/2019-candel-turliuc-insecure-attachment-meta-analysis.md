## Verified scope

Crossref confirms that this is a meta-analysis about insecure attachment and relationship satisfaction, including actor and partner associations. The title, authors, year, journal, and DOI have been verified. Effect sizes, moderators, samples, and causal claims have not yet been extracted from the full text.

## Safe current use

- Use attachment anxiety and avoidance as tentative relationship-pattern variables rather than fixed identities.
- Ask about concrete behavior from both partners before applying an attachment explanation.
- Do not use an attachment label alone to predict compatibility or recommend ending a relationship.
