## Topics to retrieve before a legal decision

- voluntary marriage and prohibition of interference or demanding property through marriage;
- spouses' personal rights and family duties;
- community property, separate property, joint debt, and marital property agreements;
- parent-child duties and protection of minors;
- divorce by agreement, litigation, cooling-off period, support, property division, and compensation;
- domestic violence, abuse, abandonment, and related civil responsibility;
- privacy, personal information, bodily rights, and valid expression of intent.

## Practical use

For marriage, housing, debt, gifts, bride price, divorce, or evidence questions, retrieve the exact current article before giving a legal conclusion. Separate four questions:

1. What happened factually?
2. What evidence exists?
3. Which legal relationship applies?
4. What action is time-sensitive or difficult to reverse?

## Limits

- A relationship recommendation and a legal conclusion are different outputs.
- Official text must be checked again when the decision depends on a deadline, amendment, local rule, or litigation strategy.
- Refer case-specific disputes to a qualified local lawyer when property, children, safety, or court procedure is materially involved.
